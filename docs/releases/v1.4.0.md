# v1.4.0 — Play, learn and keep your progress

Classic 9×9 remains the default, with a simpler place for each activity:

- **New game** holds Classic, Diagonal, Hyper and Quick 6×6, with only their supported puzzle choices. Find saved games by size, rules and puzzle.
- **Learn** holds the 4×4 introduction and 56 verified technique examples. Step through a worked example, identify a pattern in guided practice, or try an unmarked challenge.
- **Tools** holds the Classic solver, generation, imports and complete backups. Puzzle tools groups sharing, exports, printing and reset.

Players and lessons share board and candidate presentation. Notes options distinguish Fill once from live Auto-notes; Pause sits with the clock. Quick and variant games gain Check/Clear errors and resume links, with visible peer and matching-digit highlights.

Lessons apply verified deductions to the same board, support before/after comparison, and explain failed candidate premises. Completed attempts distinguish independent from assisted practice, with review and a full-puzzle transfer option. Existing content and rating policies are unchanged.

One versioned backup now includes every saved game, settings, statistics, other-board completion records, progression paths and practice history. Restore validates everything before writing and rolls back on failure. Earlier personal and single-game backups remain supported; results remain separate by activity.

This release also includes the 1.3.2 fixes for first-completion results, neutral hint wording, board sizing, practice access and documentation. Published puzzle identities, existing links and daily mappings are preserved. The optional self-hosted leaderboard continues to accept only supported Classic bank games.

Installed iPhone behavior still needs an on-device spot check; Linux WebKit emulation does not reproduce every home-screen PWA behavior.
