# v1.3.0 — Progression, technique practice and variants

- Work through Classic 9×9 from Easy to Nightmare at your own pace. Completion
  records each puzzle once and offers an explicit Next action. Small boards and
  variants have separate progression paths, resume support and backups.
- Practise 14 techniques through 56 verified positions from the existing bank.
  Choose a candidate, check the deduction and open its text and candidate map.
- Play 24 Diagonal and 24 Hyper puzzles, with the extra rules applied consistently
  to validation, solving, hints, notes, saved games, import/export, links and print.
- Generate explained small/variant puzzles in a cancellable worker.
- Reproduce practice and variant collections with offline curation tools. Benchmark
  solver correctness and transformation robustness on the bank or new input files.

Classic ratings, Daily puzzles and existing bank identities stay unchanged.
Variant ratings apply within their own rules; their collections are curated
explained puzzles, not a claim of global maximum difficulty. Practice and variants
do not submit scores to the classic leaderboard. 16×16 remains future work.

See [activities and maintenance](../activities.md) for controls, reproduction,
provenance and limitations.

Local validation covers 932 unit/DOM tests, Chromium and WebKit flows, all ten
themes, standalone/offline builds, worksheet layouts and a self-hosted upgrade
that preserves leaderboard data. The final 63 affected browser checks pass.
Independent curation reruns reproduce both collections; 800 solver/transform
benchmark cases pass, including independent solver agreement. Secret scanning
found no leaks in the public source.

Release candidate: local validation is complete; push, GitHub CI and publication
are still pending.
