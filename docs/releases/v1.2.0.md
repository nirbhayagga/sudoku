# v1.2.0 — Small classic Sudoku

- Switch between 9×9, 4×4 and 6×6. Each small size keeps its own game and completion
  progress; switching away pauses an active 9×9 game.
- Play 36 distinct minimal 4×4 puzzle classes and 120 curated 6×6 challenges.
  The 4×4 collection comes from exhaustive enumeration. The 6×6 collection is a
  selection from 2,400 seeded generations, not a claim of global maximum difficulty.
- Notes, live Auto-notes, undo/redo, pause, free hint previews and explicit reveals.
- Import, generate, share, export text or PNG, and print worksheets with optional
  answers. Save and restore a small-board game backup.
- Shared geometry defines houses, peers, symbols and rectangular boxes. Content
  identities preserve small-board progress independently of display order.

Small-board difficulty is assessed within its own size. Small games do not submit
to the 9×9 leaderboard; their backups are separate from the 9×9 Stats backup.
6×6 uses 2-row × 3-column boxes. 16×16 play and additional variants remain future
work; the tested geometry foundation does not expose an unfinished play mode.

All small-bank puzzles are checked for unique solvability, minimality, supported
logical paths and symmetry duplicates. Browser checks include touch, WebKit,
standalone disk builds, themes, imports, completion/undo and exports.
